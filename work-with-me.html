Work With Me

Community, mentorship, and focused collaboration.

I care about helping people turn complex ideas into clearer thinking and better decisions. My work is shaped by computational astrobiology, research workflows, and science communication, and I most enjoy connecting with people who are exploring, building, or figuring out their next step.

Ways to connect

I’m most interested in community, mentorship, and focused project-based collaborations.

That could mean helping someone think through a scientific or technical direction, brainstorming around a research idea, giving feedback on a project, or collaborating on something that benefits from systems thinking, scientific judgment, and clear communication.

I’m especially drawn to work that helps people understand complex ideas, make better decisions, or move from scattered possibilities toward a clearer next step.

I try to keep much of this freely available, especially for students, early-career researchers, and curious people who are still finding their path. My time and energy are limited and currently spread across multiple PhD projects, research collaborations, science communication, and mentoring — so if you’re not sure where your idea fits, just send a short note about what you’re working on and what kind of support would be most helpful.

Start a Conversation
